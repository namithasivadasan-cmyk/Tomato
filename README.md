### Project Description
It is a food delivery app that only takes orders but doesn't deliver

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
It lets us order food and satisfy our cravings without wasting money

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
Solved the problem of late night dopamine driven anticipation of ordering food without accompanying the financial guilt or caloric consequences
